## About

My broad interest is in **automatically building verification for AI across domains** — making AI reasoning in high-stakes settings machine-checkable, not just plausible.

At [Pramaana Labs](https://pramaanalabs.ai), we are extending formal verification beyond mathematics into the rule systems the real world runs on.

Before Pramaana, I spent nine years at Google — first in Google Research, then at Google DeepMind as a Staff Research Engineer working on Gemini post-training. I studied Computer Science at IIT Madras.
